Neuramid privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Neuramid is a small personal tool used only by its owner, Eslam Mohamed Ibrahim, to upload his own videos to his own TikTok account and read the stats of those videos.

It stores the access token for that one account on the owner's computer. It does not collect, sell or share data about any other TikTok user.

To remove access, revoke the app from TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Apps). Questions: open an issue at github.com/eslammohibrahim.
